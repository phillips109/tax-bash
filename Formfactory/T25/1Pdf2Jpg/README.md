# The purpose of this step is to 
1. Download the .pdf image of the form from the IRS website.
2. Convert the .pdf image to .jpg.
3. Export the .jpg image for use in subsequent stages.

The menu takes you through the steps sequentially.

# Controversy

The website used to convert the pdf to jpg is www.pdf2jpg.net.

This website sometimes shows a warning that it is not safe.

The site has its detractors and suporters.

I am not clear what is supposed to be unsafe about it.

I have been using it for over 20 years without incident.

I do not download their app. I simply convert the files and download the results.

I have tried other sites but they all complicate the process.

This site is simple and works very well.
